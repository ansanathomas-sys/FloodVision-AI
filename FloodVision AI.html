    Method and data

    Risk score = 0.4 × (3-day rainfall / 200 mm) + 0.4 × (water level relative to its danger mark) + 0.2 × low-elevation factor. Thresholds: Low < 0.35, Medium 0.35 to 0.60, High ≥ 0.60. Weights are for demonstration and are not calibrated against historical floods.

    Data: rainfall is randomly generated with a fixed seed; water level is a simple estimate from recent rainfall. Nothing here is a measurement.

    Roadmap: replace simulated inputs with Sentinel-1 radar water-extent (sees through cloud), satellite rainfall and soil moisture, and official gauge data.
